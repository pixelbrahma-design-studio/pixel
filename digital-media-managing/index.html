What is digital media managing?

Digital media managing means planning, running and measuring your social and paid channels, and Pixelbrahma handles all four parts: strategy, platform management, campaign optimisation and analytics.

The four parts of media managing

  • 01

    Strategy development

    We begin with your goals and audience, then choose channels, themes and a posting rhythm you can sustain.

    • Goal and audience definition
    • Competitor and channel review
    • A written plan with a content calendar
  • 02

    Platform management

    We keep your profiles current, scheduled and consistent in voice, and respond to your community.

    • Profile setup and optimisation
    • Scheduling and publishing
    • Comment and message monitoring
  • 03

    Campaign optimisation

    Paid campaigns are tested and refined continuously, so budget moves toward the audiences and creatives that respond.

    • Audience and creative testing
    • Budget and bid adjustments
    • Landing page recommendations
  • 04

    Analytics and insights

    Every month you receive a report that explains results in plain language and sets the next priorities.

    • Channel performance summary
    • What we learned
    • What we will do next month

Reporting explained: the KPIs we track

We agree which measures matter to your goals and report them every month.

  • Reach and impressions

    What it tells youHow many people saw your content

    Goal it supportsAwareness

  • Engagement rate

    What it tells youHow many people reacted, commented or shared

    Goal it supportsCommunity and brand affinity

  • Click-through rate

    What it tells youHow many viewers took the next step

    Goal it supportsTraffic

  • Cost per lead

    What it tells youWhat each enquiry costs in ad spend

    Goal it supportsLead generation

  • Conversion rate

    What it tells youHow many visitors became customers or enquiries

    Goal it supportsRevenue

Mistakes to avoid when planning social media management

Short, practical lessons from projects like yours.

  1. Mistake 1

    Posting without a calendar

    Do this instead

    Plan a month at a time around launches, offers and festivals so channels stay active and consistent.

  2. Mistake 2

    Ignoring comments and messages

    Do this instead

    Reply quickly in your brand voice and pass real enquiries to your sales team the same day.

  3. Mistake 3

    Boosting posts without a target

    Do this instead

    Set an audience, a budget and a goal for every paid campaign and review cost per result weekly.

Social media management FAQs

Platforms, ads, reports and onboarding, answered.

Ask us directly
Which platforms do you manage?

We agree the platforms in your strategy based on where your audience spends time. Common choices include Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn.

Do you manage ad budgets?

Yes. Ad spend is paid directly to the platform from your account, and we manage and optimise it for you.

What will I see in the monthly report?

A short summary of performance against your goals, the main lessons and the plan for the following month.

Can you work with content we already have?

Yes. We can schedule and optimise your existing content, or pair this service with our content creation team.

How much does social media management cost?

Pricing depends on the number of platforms, the posting frequency and whether paid campaigns are included. We send a written proposal after understanding your goals.

Do you run Facebook and Instagram ads?

Yes. We set up, monitor and optimise paid campaigns, test creatives and audiences and report cost per result in plain language.

Can you manage social media for businesses in Kochi, Bengaluru and Canada?

Yes. We manage channels for brands in all three regions and adapt the tone, timing and language to each audience.

What do you need from us to get started?

Your brand assets, access to your channels, a short brief on your goals and customers, and a named contact who can approve posts quickly.
