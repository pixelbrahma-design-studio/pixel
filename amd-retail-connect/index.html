About the AMD Retail Connect platform

AMD Retail Connect is a role-based platform designed by Pixelbrahma that connects retail marketing agencies with enterprise sales teams, so in-store marketing elements can be chosen, approved, ordered and tracked in one place.

Without one shared platform, ordering in-store marketing material means long email threads and spreadsheets, and enterprise sales teams struggle to see what has been requested, approved and delivered. Retail Connect brings the whole flow into a single role-based platform.

Retail marketing agencies choose the in-store elements they need, approvers review them in a clear workflow, and everyone can follow order fulfilment, budgets and regional allocation in detailed reports.

Pixelbrahma designed the screens and flows around each role, so every person who signs in sees only what they need. If your team needs a similar role-based platform for orders, approvals or reporting, we can plan it with you, from first wireframe to release.

What Retail Connect simplifies

How the platform simplifies the process for agencies and sales teams. Every project is scoped in writing first, so you know exactly what is included before any work begins.

  1. Streamlined in-store element selection: choose the marketing elements needed for each store.

  2. Effortless approval workflow: requests move to the right approver without email chains.

  3. Order fulfilment tracking: follow each order until it reaches the store.

  4. Budget control and regional allocation: keep spend within budget and divided by region.

  5. Detailed reporting: see orders, budgets and approvals in clear reports.
