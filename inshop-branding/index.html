What is in-shop branding?

In-shop branding covers the signs, displays, graphics and point-of-sale items customers see inside a store. Pixelbrahma designs them so the shopper's path from door to counter is clear and unmistakably yours.

What in-shop branding includes

  1. 01

    Store entrance and fascia graphics

  2. 02

    Wall, window and floor graphics

  3. 03

    Shelf, counter and display materials

  4. 04

    Wayfinding and price signage

  5. 05

    Production-ready files with measurements

How we plan your store graphics

  1. Step 1

    Walk the space

    We study floor plans or photos to see how customers move.

  2. Step 2

    Map the touchpoints

    We list every surface where a message can help, from window to receipt.

  3. Step 3

    Design the kit

    A coordinated set of materials is created from your brand system.

  4. Step 4

    Coordinate installation

    We supply measured artwork so your fabricator can produce and fit it accurately.

Mistakes to avoid when planning shop branding

Short, practical lessons from projects like yours.

  1. Mistake 1

    Branding the outside only

    Do this instead

    Plan the whole journey from the sign to the counter, including shelves, price cards and offers.

  2. Mistake 2

    Copying signage sizes between shops

    Do this instead

    Measure every location, because fascia, wall and window sizes rarely match.

  3. Mistake 3

    Printing offer cards that cannot be changed

    Do this instead

    Design modular offer graphics that can be swapped without redoing the display.

In-shop branding FAQs

Signage, multiple outlets and offers, answered.

Ask us directly
Do you install the signage?

We design and supply production files, and can coordinate with fabricators.

Can you brand several outlets consistently?

Yes. We create a rollout guide so every location looks the same, even when spaces differ.

Can you update seasonal offers easily?

Yes. We design swappable templates so promotions change without a full redesign.

What is in-shop branding?

It is the design of everything a customer sees and touches inside a store: signage, display graphics, price cards, packaging and wayfinding.

Do you design shop signage and display graphics?

Yes. We create fascia signs, wall graphics, window displays, price and offer cards, and supply files ready for fabrication.

Can you brand stores in different cities consistently?

Yes. We create a visual guide and master artwork so every outlet looks the same, wherever it is fitted out.
