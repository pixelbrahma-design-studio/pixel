What services does Pixelbrahma offer?

Pixelbrahma offers website design and development, mobile app development, digital marketing (social media management and content creation) and branding, including logo design, brochures, leaflets, magazines, package design, in-shop branding, outdoor advertising and TV commercials.

Do you offer web design and app development in Bengaluru and Kochi?

Yes. Our studios in Kasturinagar, Bengaluru and Kadavanthara, Kochi design and build websites and mobile apps for businesses across India.

Do you work with businesses in Canada?

Yes. Our Brampton office supports businesses in the Greater Toronto Area and across Canada with web design, app development, digital marketing and branding.

How much does a website, app or branding project cost?

The cost depends on scope, features and timeline. Send a short brief through the contact form and we will reply with questions and a price range once we understand the goal.

What does a branding project include?

A branding project can include positioning, a logo and identity system, and the materials that carry it, such as brochures, leaflets, magazines, packaging, in-shop branding, outdoor advertising and TV commercials.

Can you manage our social media and run ads?

Yes. Our digital media managing service covers strategy, platform management, campaign optimisation and monthly analytics, and our content team creates the posts, graphics and videos.

How do I start a project with Pixelbrahma?

Send a short brief using the contact form or call us. We will reply with questions and a suggested scope.

Do you design logos and brand identity?

Yes. Our logo design service creates a logo, colour palette, typography and a short usage guide, and we can extend the identity into brochures, packaging, signage and more.

Can you create brochures, leaflets and packaging?

Yes. We design brochures, leaflets, magazines and product packaging, and supply print-ready artwork for your printer.

Do you offer outdoor advertising and TV commercial production?

Yes. We design hoardings, banners and vehicle branding, and we develop and guide the production of TV commercials and video ads.

What is Smolgoodi?

Smolgoodi is a product built by Pixelbrahma that helps home sellers in India list products and take orders through a shareable link, with orders arriving on WhatsApp.
