## Days 1 to 14: foundations

1. **Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.** Verify the domain and submit `sitemap.xml`. Record a baseline: indexed pages, top queries, top pages.
2. **Rankings baseline.** Record positions for each primary keyword in SEO-STRATEGY.md (before migration if the old site is still live).
3. **Google Business Profile for each office** (Bengaluru, Kochi). Verification is required, so start now because postcards or video calls can take days.
   - Primary category: *Website designer* or *Marketing agency*. Add secondary categories such as *Graphic designer*, *Advertising agency* and *Software company* only where they truly apply to that office.
   - Services: list the three services and the 12 sub-services.
   - Hours, phone and website URL (use the contact page or home page, consistently).
   - Photos: office exterior and interior, team, work samples. Real photos only.
   - Q&A: seed 5 genuine questions and answers per profile (pricing process, timelines, remote work).
   - Posts: one post per week (project snippet, tip, offer).
4. **Apple Business Connect and Bing Places.** Import from Google Business Profile once verified.
5. **Facebook and YouTube.** Confirm they use the consistent bio and link to the site. Add the channel trailer.
6. **Smolgoodi credit link.** Ask the Smolgoodi site to add "Built by Pixelbrahma" linking to https://pixelbrahma.com/.

### Review-request template

Rules: ask every client, never offer incentives, never write reviews for them, and reply to every review within a few days.

## Days 30 to 90: backlinks and content

### Backlink strategy (earned, relevant, white-hat)

1. **Case-study features.** For each real client project (with permission), publish a case study on the site and ask the client to link to it from their site or announcement.
2. **Guest articles.** Pitch 1 to 2 per month to design, startup and marketing publications in India and Canada. Include one contextual link to the relevant service page, not the home page every time.
3. **Directories and awards.** Complete Clutch and GoodFirms profiles with verified reviews. Apply to genuine design awards only if the work qualifies.
4. **Partnerships.** Printers, photographers, hosting providers and complementary agencies can link to each other's "partners" pages.
5. **PR and local press.** Announce the new website, notable launches and Smolgoodi milestones to local business and tech press in Bengaluru, Kochi and the Greater Toronto Area.
6. **Resource pages.** Publish a genuinely useful guide (see article ideas) and request inclusion on relevant resource lists.
7. **Reclaim mentions.** If anyone mentions Pixelbrahma without a link, politely ask for one.

Avoid: paid link networks, comment spam, private blog networks, and exact-match anchor text overload.

## Reputation and monitoring

- Set up Google Alerts for "Pixelbrahma" and "Pixelbrahma Design Studio". Review monthly.
- Respond to every review and mention, positive or negative, professionally.
- Keep a shared sheet of reviews received per platform and per office.

## Analytics and migration monitoring

1. **GA4** (consent-gated): set the measurement ID, mark form submissions and phone-link clicks as key events.
2. **Pre-launch:** export the old site's top pages, queries and backlinks (Search Console, Ahrefs or Semrush if available).
3. **Launch week (daily):** check Search Console Coverage and Crawl Stats, 404s, and the redirect map using the old URL list.
4. **Weeks 2 to 4 (weekly):** compare indexed pages, impressions and rankings with the baseline. Fix any URL that returns 404 but had traffic or links.
5. **Month 2 to 3 (monthly):** review Core Web Vitals in Search Console, new backlinks, and review count.
